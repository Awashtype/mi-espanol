Grammar colors

Muted color shows the tense/form family. A wavy underline marks subjunctive, an outline marks imperative, and tiny labels indicate perfect or progressive constructions. Grammar cues appear only after you reveal the answer.

A verb’s form does not always describe when an event happens: enviara is an imperfect subjunctive form in a present polite request. Fixed expressions use a separate warm underline: sin darme cuenta. Grammar markings are added only when a card has verified labels; an unmarked verb isn't necessarily unimportant.
